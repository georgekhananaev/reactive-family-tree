# Background melodies

Five melodies to choose from for the tree's music, each in one of the five-note
scales of Japanese music. Listen to them here, then pick under "Manage family",
"Settings", "Music", where each choice also has a Listen button. The melody in
the background and the quick tune for the Play button are chosen apart, so they
can be two different ones.

| File | Melody | What it is |
|------|--------|------------|
| `1-morning-mist.mp3` | Morning mist | A flute over a rolling koto. Warm and unhurried |
| `2-koto-garden.mp3` | Koto in the garden | A koto alone, with room between the notes. The old, wistful scale |
| `3-bamboo-wind.mp3` | Bamboo wind | A bamboo flute, slow and breathing, with a deep string now and then |
| `4-water-garden.mp3` | Water garden | Drops ringing in a water bell, and a string far off. The quietest one |
| `5-island-song.mp3` | Island song | A lilting tune in the bright scale of the southern islands |

Each has a second file ending in `-years-playing`. That is what the same melody
turns into while the Play button walks the tree through the years: quicker, with
a drum, and it grows as the family does. It ends on the chord that brings the
calm melody back.

## What these files are

Nothing here is a recording. Every note is worked out by the app itself, from a
score written in `packages/react/src/music.ts`: a plucked koto string, a bamboo
flute and a water bell, each made from arithmetic. These files are that same
music, played once and saved, so they are free to keep, change and ship.

They are turned up for listening. On the tree the music plays more softly, under
a conversation and not over one.

The app does not use these files. They are here to choose from, and can be
deleted once a melody is chosen.
