# Code of conduct

This project is about families, and it should be a kind place to work.

## What we ask

- Be patient and respectful, in issues, pull requests, and reviews.
- Assume good intent, and ask before you conclude otherwise.
- Criticise the work, never the person.
- Welcome people who are new, to the project or to programming.
- Remember that family history touches adoption, loss, estrangement, and identity.
  Treat real examples with care, and never post details of living people.

## What is not accepted

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Remarks about someone's gender, sexuality, race, religion, age, disability,
  family situation, or background.
- Sharing anyone's private information without their say-so.
- Unwelcome sexual attention.
- Anything else that would be out of place among considerate colleagues.

## If something goes wrong

Tell the maintainer privately, through the contact options on their GitHub
profile. Say what happened and where. Reports are kept in confidence.

The maintainer may remove comments, close threads, or block someone from the
project when that is what it takes to keep it a decent place. Decisions aim to be
fair and proportionate, and a first mistake made in good faith gets a quiet word,
not a ban.

This applies everywhere the project lives: the repository, its discussions, and
anywhere someone is speaking for it.
